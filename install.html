About Money Review

Add Money Review to Claude.

Three steps, about two minutes. You do this once, on any computer, tablet or phone. After that, Money Review is ready whenever you open Claude.

Install link last checked: Nov 18, 2026 · working

STEP 1

Download the skill file

One zip file, about 1.2 MB. Leave it zipped. Claude reads what's inside.

Download the skill

Saved to your Downloads folder as a .zip file. On a Mac with Safari it may open into a folder: see the fix below.

STEP 2

Open Claude's upload page

It opens claude.ai in your browser. Sign in if asked. A free account works. The page is called "Upload a skill" or "Upload a plugin".

Open the upload page

Opens claude.ai, which is run by Anthropic.

STEP 3

Pick the file and upload

Tap the dashed box, choose the zip from your Downloads, then tap Upload. On a phone you may need to scroll down to see the button. Wait a few seconds. You're done when the name money-review appears.

Asked "Replace money-review skill?" You already have it. Tap Upload and replace to update.

Then start your review.

Open Claude, in the app or the browser, and start a new chat. Type this line and send. Begin with the Brooks practice family before using your own statements.

/money-review

If something doesn't work

The button opened the Claude app. The app can't add a skill. Copy this address and paste it into Safari, Chrome or another browser:

claude.ai/customize/skills/new/upload

Your download opened into a folder. Safari on a Mac does this. In your Downloads, right-click the money-review folder and choose Compress. Upload the .zip file it makes. Don't upload the SKILL.md file from inside the folder.

The page looks different. In the browser at claude.ai, open the menu, choose Customize or Plugins, then Add, then Upload.

Claude can't make your spreadsheet. Open Settings, then Capabilities, and turn on file creation.
