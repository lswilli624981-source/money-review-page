How it works

Three steps. Claude leads the way.

  1. Add the skill

    A skill is an add-on that teaches Claude this review. First, turn on file creation: if Claude shows a Create files and artifacts window, click Try it out; otherwise check Settings → Capabilities. Then go to Customize → Skills → + Add → Upload skill and choose the .zip file as it is. One time only. The Quick Start shows every screen.

  2. Start the review

    Start a new chat (or a Project called "My Money Review"). Type /money-review, or type: "Start my money review using the money-review skill."

  3. Follow Claude's lead

    It walks you through everything, one step at a time: practice or real, the questions, what to upload, then your results and the sit-down.

For your own review, allow an evening to download statements first. On Claude's free plan, a 6-month review usually takes two or three sittings because of usage limits, and that's normal. It goes best if you add the skill first, then start the review in a new chat when you have a fresh allowance. Nothing is lost between sittings: come back to the same chat and type "Please continue where you left off." Built for everyday household money: paychecks, bills, cards, savings and personal debt. Not for business finances, real estate investing, trusts or estates.

Your data stays yours

I never see your statements. Neither does Cedric or the Challenge.

It stays in your Claude account

Your statements and results stay in your chat, which Anthropic (the company behind Claude) handles under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Larry, Cedric Nash or the Black Wealth Growth Challenge.

Only your own accounts

Review your own accounts, or joint accounts where both of you agree. Remove or cover full account numbers; the last 4 digits are enough. Claude masks any full number it finds.

No passwords, ever

Claude will never ask for logins, passwords or PINs. Never share them.

Delete it when you're done

You can delete the chat (and the Project) whenever you like. To keep your chats from being used to train Claude, turn off Help Improve our AI models in Settings → Privacy. Setup walks you through it.

Other people stay anonymous

Payments to family and friends are labeled by role, never by name or memo. Practice mode needs nothing real at all.

Education, not advice

Investment, tax and legal questions go on a list for a licensed professional. The coach won't answer them.

If money is weighing on you, the review will pause the numbers and point you to real help (U.S. numbers):
  • 988 · Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text. In immediate danger, call 911.
  • 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738) · National Problem Gambling Helpline. Family members can call too.
  • 1-800-799-7233 · National Domestic Violence Hotline. Or text START to 88788, or chat at thehotline.org.
  • nfcc.org · a nonprofit credit counselor, for debt you can't keep up with.
  • 211 · if you can't cover food, rent or utilities right now.
The review isn't counseling. For your relationship or your feelings about money, a licensed counselor or a trusted faith leader is the right person to talk with.

Common questions

Before you start.

What does it cost?

The kit is free. It runs in your own Claude account. Claude has a free plan; a paid plan gets through a review in fewer sittings.

Is the coach Cedric Nash?

No. The coach is Claude, an AI, teaching from the ideas in Cedric's book. It says so before it starts.

Will it tell me what to invest in?

No. It's education. Investment, tax and legal questions go on a list for a licensed professional.

Do I need to be good with computers?

If you can attach a file to an email, you can do this. The Quick Start shows every screen.

What if I stop partway?

Nothing is lost. Come back to the same chat and type "Please continue where you left off."

Should I start with my real statements?

Start with the Brooks family. Once you've seen how it works, run it on your own.
